How to progress

  • Double progression: when you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add a little weight next time. The app tells you when.
  • Stop most sets with 1–2 reps left in the tank. Take the last set close to failure.
  • Rest 2–3 minutes on big lifts and 60–90 seconds on smaller ones. Tick a set and the rest timer starts.
  • Log every set so you can beat last week. Leave weight blank for bodyweight. Tap “Save backup” every week or two.
  • Every 6–8 weeks, take a lighter week, cutting sets about in half.
  • Swap any exercise that causes pain for a similar one.
